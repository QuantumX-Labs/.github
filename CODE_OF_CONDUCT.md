## 1. Our Pledge

At **QuantumX Labs**, we believe innovation thrives in an environment built on mutual respect, collaboration, integrity, and inclusivity.

As an independent technology research and development initiative, we are committed to creating a professional and welcoming community where developers, engineers, researchers, students, and technology enthusiasts can collaborate, exchange ideas, and contribute to meaningful technological advancements.

We are committed to providing a positive experience for everyone, regardless of their background, experience level, technical expertise, or identity.

Every member of our community has a responsibility to maintain these standards.

## 2. Our Values

Our community is built around the following principles:

- **Respect:** Treat every individual with dignity and professionalism.
- **Inclusivity:** Welcome people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.
- **Collaboration:** Encourage knowledge sharing, teamwork, and constructive discussions.
- **Innovation:** Support creativity, experimentation, and intellectual curiosity.
- **Integrity:** Be honest, transparent, and accountable in your contributions.
- **Excellence:** Maintain high standards of technical quality and professional conduct.
- **Responsibility:** Promote ethical technology development and responsible research.

## 3. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome constructive feedback and alternative perspectives.
- Provide helpful and actionable technical feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience and expertise.
- Encourage newcomers and support their learning.
- Acknowledge the contributions of others.
- Respect intellectual property and licensing requirements.
- Participate in technical discussions constructively.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Prioritize collaboration over unnecessary competition.

We encourage an environment where people can question ideas, challenge technical decisions, and disagree respectfully without making discussions personal.

## 4. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable within the QuantumX Labs community:

### Harassment and Discrimination
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Threatening or abusive communication.

### Unprofessional Conduct
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated hostile arguments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Personal insults, humiliation, or targeted harassment.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable community guidelines.

### Technical Misconduct
- Submitting malicious code or intentionally harmful software.
- Unauthorized access to repositories, systems, or infrastructure.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of another person's work.
- Deliberate sabotage of projects or contributions.
- Misuse of organizational resources.
- Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information.

### Research and Security Misconduct
- Conducting unauthorized security testing.
- Misusing research environments or datasets.
- Distributing malicious tools or harmful payloads.
- Violating privacy or applicable data protection requirements.

## 5. Professional Collaboration

QuantumX Labs encourages collaboration across multiple technology disciplines, including:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
- Software Engineering and Application Development.
- Cybersecurity and Network Security.
- Cloud Computing and DevOps.
- Data Engineering and Analytics.
- Emerging and Experimental Technologies.

Contributors should maintain professional standards while working on projects, research activities, technical discussions, and collaborative initiatives.

Technical disagreements should be resolved through evidence, constructive discussion, and engineering reasoning.

## 6. Responsible Technology Development

We believe technological innovation must be accompanied by ethical responsibility.

Community members are encouraged to:

- Respect user privacy and data protection principles.
- Develop secure and reliable software.
- Use datasets and third-party resources responsibly.
- Evaluate potential risks associated with AI systems.
- Follow responsible security research practices.
- Respect applicable laws, licenses, and regulations.
- Avoid developing or distributing technology intended to facilitate harmful activities.

Innovation should create value without compromising the safety, privacy, or rights of others.

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the QuantumX Labs maintainers.

Reports may be submitted through the organization's officially designated private communication channels.

When submitting a report, include:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant dates and context.
- Links to relevant discussions or contributions, where applicable.
- Any supporting evidence you can safely provide.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

All reports will be reviewed with appropriate discretion.

## 8. Enforcement Responsibilities

QuantumX Labs maintainers are responsible for reviewing reported violations and determining appropriate responses.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

| Level | Enforcement Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Warning and request for corrective behaviour |
| 2 | Temporary restriction from discussions or contributions |
| 3 | Temporary suspension of repository or community access |
| 4 | Permanent removal from relevant community spaces |

The maintainers may take immediate action when necessary to protect community members, projects, and organizational resources.

Enforcement decisions will consider the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of the violation.

## 9. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all QuantumX Labs organizational spaces, including:

- Official GitHub repositories.
- Issues and Pull Requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Official communication channels.
- Organization-sponsored events and activities.
- Other spaces where individuals represent QuantumX Labs in an official capacity.

Individual repositories may adopt additional community requirements where appropriate.

## 10. Community Commitment

Every contributor plays an important role in shaping the QuantumX Labs ecosystem.

We encourage everyone to contribute responsibly, support fellow developers, share knowledge, and help create an environment where innovation can flourish.

Our community is stronger when people collaborate with respect, curiosity, and integrity.
